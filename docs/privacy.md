# Privacy: recordings sent from tilawa.dev/help

This policy applies to recordings sent with consent version `2026-11-report-1` (in force from 12 November 2026).

This page explains what happens to a recording you send from [tilawa.dev/help](https://tilawa.dev/help/). It is written to be read in a couple of minutes. If anything is unclear, email help@tilawa.dev.

## The short version

- Nothing is sent unless you tick the first box and press Send.
- We store your recording privately. Only the Tilawa team can listen to it.
- No name or email is attached to it.
- You can delete it at any time with the code we show you.
- Using it to train models, or putting it in a public dataset, are separate choices. Both are off unless you tick them.

## Tilawa in an app is different

The Tilawa engine that follows your recitation runs on your own device. When you use the [tilawa.dev demo](https://tilawa.dev/), or an app built with Tilawa, your audio is not uploaded. Only the contributor page at tilawa.dev/help sends recordings, and only when you choose to. Other apps that use Tilawa have their own privacy policies.

## What you agree to

Before you start, the page asks:

| Choice | Wording | |
|---|---|---|
| Send | "Send this recording and what the app showed to the Tilawa team so we can find and fix mistakes. It is stored privately. You can delete it later with the code we give you." | Required to send |
| Training | "Also use it to train and test future Tilawa models." | Optional, unticked |
| Public dataset | "Also include it in a public research dataset (CC BY 4.0)." | Optional, unticked |

Your choices are saved with the recording, together with the version of this wording you saw.

## What we store

When you press Send, we store:

- **Your recording**: the audio of your take, up to 5 minutes, as the app heard it.
- **What the app showed**: where it thought you were in the Quran, and any warnings it raised, with their times.
- **Your answers**: the moments you marked, your answers to our questions, and anything you typed (for example, what you actually recited).
- **Your choices**: the three consent choices above and the wording version.
- **A random contributor code**: a random id your browser keeps (in its local storage) so we can tell that several recordings came from the same voice. It is not linked to your name, email or account. Clearing this site's data in your browser makes a new one.
- **Technical details**: the versions of the Tilawa engine and model, whether it ran on your graphics chip or processor, and when we received the recording.

We do **not** store your name, your email address, your IP address or your browser details with the recording. To limit abuse, the server counts uploads per network address for an hour, in memory only; that count is never saved with recordings.

If you sign up for email updates, your email goes on a separate list. It is never linked to your recordings.

## Who can access it

- Your recording is private. It is not published and there is no public link to it.
- Members of the Tilawa team can listen to it and read what was sent, to find and fix the engine's mistakes. Access needs an admin login.
- Automated review tools that we run may process the recording to sort and prioritise reports. No outside service receives your recording for this; if that changes, this policy and its version change first.
- If you ticked **Training**, the recording may be used to train and test future Tilawa models.
- If you ticked **Public dataset**, the recording and your answers may be published in a research dataset under the [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence, which lets anyone reuse it with attribution.

## How long we keep it

- You can delete your recording at any time with the delete code shown after sending. It is removed from review and from future dataset exports at once.
- Private recordings that haven't been reviewed are deleted after 24 months.
- Recordings you delete are removed straight away from everything the team can see. Their audio is then purged from storage within 30 days.
- Backups of the server may hold a deleted recording until they are rotated out within the same window.

## Deleting your recording

After you send a recording, the page shows a **delete code** once. Keep it if you might want the recording deleted later.

- Press **Delete this recording now** on that page, or
- email the code to help@tilawa.dev and we will delete it.

If you've lost the code, email us anyway with the date and time you recorded, and we'll do our best to find it.

What deletion can't undo:

- If the recording was already used to train a model, that model isn't retrained. We stop using the recording for any new training.
- If the recording was already published in a public dataset, copies that others downloaded can't be recalled. We remove it from future versions of the dataset.

## Children

Please don't record yourself if you are under 16 unless a parent or guardian agrees.

## Recordings sent before November 2026

Recordings sent from the earlier version of tilawa.dev/help were made under consent version `2026-09-30`. That wording was:

> "I agree that my recordings are released under CC BY 4.0 and may be used to train and evaluate speech models and published as a research dataset. I'm 18 or older, or have a guardian's permission."

Those recordings keep the consent given at the time. They are not re-labelled under the new choices. To have one deleted, email help@tilawa.dev with the date and time you recorded.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the consent wording gets a new version. Recordings keep the version their contributor agreed to.
