# Help Tilawa learn: recite at tilawa.dev/help

Tilawa is a free engine that Quran apps can use to follow your recitation, ayah by ayah, and notice memorization mistakes. It learns where it goes wrong from real people reciting on real phones. That's where you can help.

It takes a few minutes. You need a phone or computer with a microphone.

## How it works

1. **Open [tilawa.dev/help](https://tilawa.dev/help/).** The page is in English and Arabic.
2. **Read the short explanation and choose what you agree to.** Sending is required. Training and the public dataset are optional and start unticked. See [what each one means](privacy.md#what-you-agree-to).
3. **Press Start and recite.** Recite any part of the Quran, the way you normally do, for up to 5 minutes. You don't need to choose a surah: Tilawa finds your place. The first time, it takes a moment to get ready.
4. **If you stumble, press "I stumbled here"** and keep going. It doesn't interrupt you.
5. **Press Stop.**
6. **Answer a few short questions.** We play back a few moments, one at a time, and ask one simple question about each, for example "Did you make a mistake here?" or "Did we show the wrong ayah?". You can always answer "Not sure" or skip. You can also mark a moment yourself while listening back.
7. **Press Send.** Keep the delete code we show you if you might want the recording deleted later.

## Please recite naturally

Recite as you normally would. Please don't make mistakes on purpose: natural recitation, with its natural slips, is what helps most. A recitation with no mistakes at all is just as useful, because it shows us where Tilawa raises a warning it shouldn't.

## Tips

- A quiet room helps. Your phone's microphone is all you need.
- If the browser asks for the microphone, choose Allow. If you blocked it by accident, the page shows how to allow it again.
- If sending fails, the page keeps your recording and lets you try again. You can choose to keep it on your device for 7 days, so you can send it later even after closing the page.

## What happens to your recording

The Tilawa team listens to the moments that matter, checks what the engine got right and wrong, and uses that to fix it. Your recording is stored privately, with no name or email attached. The full details are in the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
