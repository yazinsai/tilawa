# Contributor invitation

**Draft.** For a short message, email or post inviting people to recite at tilawa.dev/help. Not for posting without approval.

Before sending: check that tilawa.dev/help is live with consent version `2026-11-report-1`.

## English

> **Help a Quran app listen better**
>
> We're building Tilawa, a free tool that Quran apps can use to follow your recitation and gently point out a missed or changed word.
>
> It learns from real people. Would you recite for a few minutes?
>
> 1. Open **tilawa.dev/help** on your phone or computer.
> 2. Press Start and recite any part of the Quran, the way you normally do. You don't need to choose a surah.
> 3. Press Stop and answer a few short questions about what Tilawa showed.
>
> Your recording is kept private, with no name or email attached, and you can delete it any time with the code we give you. Using it for training is your choice.
>
> Every recitation helps, including ones with no mistakes at all. Jazakum Allahu khayran.

Short version (for chat):

> Could you spare 5 minutes to help a free Quran app learn? Recite anything you know at tilawa.dev/help, then answer a few quick questions. Private, and you can delete it any time.
