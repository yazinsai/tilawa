Tilawa · open recitation dataset

Make a mistake on purpose.

We're building an offline app that listens to your recitation and gently points out mistakes. To teach it, we need recordings of real mistakes: skipped words, wrong harakat, a missed ghunna. Read the ayah, make the mistake shown, submit. About 20 seconds each.

Before you start

  • Use a quiet room. Phone or laptop mic is fine — we want both.
  • Don't say your name or anything personal. We never ask who you are.
  • Each prompt tells you exactly which mistake to make, highlighted in the text. About 1 in 5 asks you to read correctly.
