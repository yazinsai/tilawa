## Concrete next step (proposal only)

Pilot **50–100 hours of Muaalem** from at least5 training reciters, chosen after overlap checks. Inspect100 clips for pause boundaries, repeated words and phoneme/convention consistency. Compare a fixed-compute tlog-only arm with an arm drawing20% of training audio from the expert source;20% is a proposed starting mix, not a proven ratio. Keep held-out learner speakers and all gold clips excluded. Check clean phone recognition and checker false warnings alongside real substitutions, omissions and30 existing vowel sites. Lower phoneme error with fewer audible mistakes detected is a failed pilot.

Before any import, build a source-recording and reciter inventory across QUD, Muaalem, EveryAyah and zaibihassan. The same professional may recur under different spellings, styles, bitrates and segmentations; file hashes alone miss such overlap. Split by person and recording identity, deduplicate acoustically, and keep all benchmark originals and splice donors out. Do not add the advertised hours together until this is done. No source above is established as a replacement for tlog's crowd phone conditions.
