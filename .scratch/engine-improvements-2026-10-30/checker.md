# Checker improvements: reduce false accusations without hiding missed mistakes

Research note, 30 October 2026. No engine, label, benchmark or scoreboard changes were made. Scope is v1 memorization errors; these proposals do not add pronunciation assessment.

The best next experiment is **bounded, boundary-aware confirmation**, followed by **assignment-specific anchoring**. Increasing `minConfidence` or `SUPPORT` alone is unlikely to solve the observed failures. Occurrence state and precise diagnosis deserve separate changes, because a misplaced/class-mismatched real warning is different from falsely accusing correct recitation.

## What the evidence establishes

- The current checker already compares two CTC sequence scores over the suspect word and one recited neighbor on each side. It requires the decoded alternative to beat the written sequence by 10 nats. It sums CTC timing paths, not just one forced alignment. Suggestions to “add posterior confirmation” would repeat shipped work. The historical development sweep first lost a true hit at 16 nats; omissions were the hits lost at higher margins. At 10, student declared results were 56 hits / 8 false positives / 8 misses. See [local-window-v1](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/docs/benchmarks/local-window-v1.md).
- The longer stitched experiment still produced 217 wrong/added warnings in 743 minutes, 1.46/5 min. Disabling free-session finalization changed none of those warnings. This is not mainly a session-end problem. Those streams concatenate per-clip evidence and often change speakers, so they are useful regression material, not representative continuous learner sessions. See [stitched-warnings-v1](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/docs/benchmarks/stitched-warnings-v1.md).
- The newer, local, untracked [expanded QUD report](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/docs/benchmarks/session-qud-int8-expanded-v1.json) is a better continuous-audio diagnostic than the historical three-recording table. Supported-style `none` has **69 clean warnings / 195.689 clean minutes = 1.763/5 min**, from 11 recordings/9 speakers. Its 79 total warnings are a different numerator. Assignment has **99 / 110.754 = 4.469/5 min** in six recordings. Compare the same six at `none`: **41 / 110.754 = 1.851/5 min**. Assignment adds 58 warnings: Luhaidan–Fatir contributes +52 (19 to 71), Mandour–Taha +6 (9 to 15); the other four do not change. This strongly motivates a paired context investigation, rather than claiming assignment always hurts. One recording fails evidence matching, four hadr recordings are explicitly unsupported, and there are no positive error labels here.
- Seven saved [human findings](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/.scratch/help-review/human-findings.json) distinguish real problems. Correct **من** at 114:4:1 and **حاشرين** at 7:111:7 were falsely warned with student confidences about .830 and .855. The teacher also falsely warns on من with confidence .999. Two الجنة-for-الجحيم substitutions were real, but their warnings started at the preceding فإن. The omitted مع at 94:5:2 was called a wrong word; an أخوهم omission was missing from its label. These are not seven independent false accusations. The last two boundary/class issues can count as both an FP and FN under exact matching.
- The [automatic triage](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/.scratch/help-review/automated-triage.json) has 9 reviewed incidents and 16 needing an independent audio opinion. “Teacher agrees” is correlated evidence: distillation/shared model family and reference conventions can reproduce the same error. Existing teacher agreement, automated alignment and text inspection cannot establish audio truth.
- The last complete scoreboard history is 13 October. Later rows with nulls mean measurements were not supplied, not zero performance or a newly demonstrated failure. QUD remains reported-only; the [session protocol](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/.scratch/session-bench/spec.md) requires separate recall, warning delay, non-error and occurrence measures before promotion.

## Ranked candidates

### 1. Make posterior confirmation robust to boundary choice, then calibrate the decision by error class

**Mechanism.** `supported()` chooses audio endpoints from a single edit alignment and compares the raw reference with one greedy-decoded alternative. CTC marginalizes timing *inside those endpoints*, but cannot recover a relevant sound excluded by the endpoint choice. The alternative is selected from the same posterior tensor being used to verify it, so a large score difference is not independent corroboration. The current mean decoded-token confidence is confidence in what the listener emitted, not calibrated probability that the reciter made a memorization mistake. `skipped_passage` bypasses this check entirely. [Code](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/packages/core/src/checker.ts) (`clarity`, `supported`, `decide`); [CTC scoring](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/packages/core/src/ctc.ts).

**Small experiment.** First run a read-only candidate logger on frozen existing evidence. For every candidate record: class, raw ΔNLL, reference/alternative length, frames, number of edited consonants, top-k missing-reference rate, posterior entropy, alignment boundary changes and whether it is near a pause/repeat/anchor. Compute ΔNLL for the existing window and at most two expanded windows ending/starting at a stable neighboring word; do not sweep dozens of windows. Test a conservative rule: confirm only when the decoded alternative remains better across the predeclared plausible windows. Retain raw and length-adjusted ΔNLL as competing features; normalization is an empirical ablation, not automatically a better likelihood ratio. Then compare a very small class-specific calibrator (regularized logistic model or a monotonic two-feature table) against a single-threshold baseline at matched positive recall. Avoid a neural confidence model on this sample size.

**Minimal seam.** Extract the pure hypothesis/window scoring portion of `supported(issue)` into an internal helper returning scores plus evidence coverage; keep public events unchanged. Integrate only at `decide()`. `PosteriorFrames` already supports the necessary scoring and top-k membership checks. Add explicit “no posterior coverage” tracing: returning null currently permits confirmation, which should not silently count as verified support.

**Success.** On locked validation, at unchanged correctly detected errors, reduce clean confirmations by at least 20% overall and improve at least two speaker/source groups; report whether this comes from the window rule or calibration. Keep the original declared 56/64 and lead-free 53/64 hits as regression floors, including each original correctly detected omission. Report any newly caught errors separately, so gains cannot disguise losing a different existing hit. This is an experiment acceptance target, not a new product gate.

**Reject.** Gains vanish on expanded windows, depend on one reciter, or come only from converting real errors to `uncertain`. Reject a fitted rule if its held-out discrimination is no better than ΔNLL alone at matched recall. If top-5 uncertainty determines most outcomes, request top-k/full-logit evidence on a bounded subset before adding confidence complexity. Do not treat an arbitrary 10-nat gap as a calibrated posterior probability.

**Risk.** Requiring agreement across windows can lose short genuine omissions or words next to another error. Keep these as protected slices. Summing related chunk scores or multiplying teacher/student probabilities would falsely count correlated evidence twice.

### 2. Give declared passages an acoustic anchor-quality check independent of global tracker lock

**Mechanism.** Explicit assignment may align from `passage.from` before any tracker lock. `decide()` allows `declared && !latched` to bypass `trackerLocked`; a nonlocked tracker does not drop a declared anchor. This is intentional for genuine substitutions that send the global tracker elsewhere, but means a poor local anchor can still generate confident omissions/wrong words. A fixed-start path also treats a partial start or repeat differently from free latching. The +58 paired QUD warnings provide a concrete target, not proof that this mechanism caused all 58. [Code](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/packages/core/src/checker.ts) (`anchorOn`, `track`, `decide`).

**Small experiment.** Replay the six paired recordings and log every assignment-only candidate against: tracker state, recent local alignment cost, competing start/return points, anchor age and repeat boundary. Classify the +58 before changing thresholds. Compare the current behavior with a local support guard that requires stable correctly aligned flanks within the declared passage, allowing the target word itself to disagree. If the start is unresolved, hold the candidate pending until flanks arrive; bound the hold by one subsequent word or a predeclared short timeout. Do **not** require the global tracker to accept the declared passage: that would suppress the exact mutashabihat errors assignment is intended to catch.

**Minimal seam.** Add an internal local-anchor support result to `judge()`/`evaluate()` and consult it in `decide()`. Preserve `passage` as the intended text. Separate “assignment tells us where the user meant to recite” from “this audio is locally anchored well enough to accuse a word.”

**Success.** Reduce assignment's excess over paired `none` by at least half on development, then show no new assignment penalty on held-out speakers and no regression on correctly detected declared mutashabihat substitutions, start errors, omissions or deliberate out-and-back jumps. Compare against the same recordings, not 6-assignment versus 11-none aggregates. Preserve unchanged tracker events in this checker-only experiment.

**Reject.** It works only by requiring tracker lock, suppresses errors at assignment start, or makes declared recall collapse toward the free arm. If most extra warnings have strong stable flanks, this is the wrong mechanism; pursue candidate 1 or 3 instead.

**Risk.** The currently available assignment controls contain few warnings outside the two development reciters. A spectacular development reduction is not evidence of broad generalization.

### 3. Use a narrow, reviewed set of legal reference variants in confirmation

**Mechanism.** The edit path makes held sounds cheap (`SOFT`/`HELD`), but `supported()` sends the unmodified phoneme string into CTC. Tracker posterior scoring already uses `collapseHeld`; checker posterior scoring does not. Thus alignment tolerances and acoustic confirmation encode different assumptions. Continuous joining and waqf may add other legitimate differences. The hadr analysis supports investigating this mismatch but does not establish which written sounds are genuinely absent. [Fuzzy costs](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/packages/core/src/fuzzy.ts), [checker](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/packages/core/src/checker.ts), [fast-recitation issue](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/.scratch/session-bench/issues/09-fast-recitation.md).

**Small experiment.** Restrict the first ablation to bounded held-run alternatives already justified by the v1 phoneme contract. Preserve consonant identity, lexical boundaries and minimal gemination; enumerate a small canonical/shortened set on both the written and decoded hypotheses. Compare scored *sets* rather than unilaterally deleting reference tokens. Audit the top changes with an independent qualified listener before extending to waqf/wasl variants. Any variant that removes a lexical consonant or lets a different word pass needs an explicit reviewed linguistic rule, not a global edit discount. Keep hadr as a separate diagnostic slice, not silently supported.

**Minimal seam.** A pure internal `confirmationVariants(text, boundaryContext)` feeding candidate 1's scoring helper. Do not rewrite the shared reference asset or listener target inventory for the first experiment. Distinct strings must be deduplicated; summing variants needs declared class priors or otherwise controlled set size, not an unbounded “best of many” advantage for the intended answer.

**Success.** A paired ablation reduces confirmations specifically on reviewed legitimate variants and preserves all protected lexical substitutions/omissions, including shadda-sensitive and short-word slices. It must improve more than one reciter at matched error recall. Report raw versus variant score and the licensing rule for each affected warning.

**Reject.** The benefit requires accepting missing hard consonants, weakens lexical distinction, or disappears when the same variant budget is applied to both hypotheses. “Both teacher and student omit it” is insufficient evidence to license a variant.

**Risk.** Overpermissive variants can erase real mistakes. This is a memorization-tolerance experiment; it cannot establish pronunciation correctness or justify general hadr support.

### 4. Track error decisions by recitation occurrence, with bounded evidence-based confirmation

**Mechanism.** `open`, `decided`, `last` and `right` are keyed by Quran word index. Once a word is decided it is never reopened; any correct visit in the current window protects that word from a later wrong visit. That is useful for self-correction, but conflates separate recitations of the same location. The session scorer now distinguishes occurrences; the engine does not. A fixed elapsed 2.5 s can also confirm an unstable path, while repeated evaluations of overlapping evidence add no independent information. [Code](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/packages/core/src/checker.ts) (`judge`, `evaluate`, `commit`); [session matching](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/.scratch/session-bench/spec.md).

**Small experiment.** Build a tiny deterministic corpus of occurrence sequences before runtime changes: correct→repeat-wrong, wrong→self-correct, wrong→confirm→later correct, correct→restart-correct, omission→recover-before-horizon, and identical twin ayahs. Replay each with realistic cached evidence where available and synthetic phonemes as state-machine unit checks only. Assign an internal occurrence/episode key on a stable return/restart, not on every noisy `OP_JUMP`; retain the relationship between a pending error and its correction. Require confirmation stability across a bounded amount of genuinely new right-context evidence, not merely N overlapping calls. Store the original candidate evidence so later window shifts cannot silently change its occurrence.

**Minimal seam.** Internal occurrence identity in `Visit`/`Issue`/`Open` and the maps; preserve public word locations and warning IDs. Keep exact lifecycle behavior in existing [checker tests](/Users/rock/ai/projects/tilawa/packages/core/test/checker.test.ts), especially short/long repeats and recoverable skips.

**Success.** Every occurrence corpus case has the intended ID, class and lifecycle; clean repeat/restart warnings do not increase on QUD, and a real error on a later independent repetition remains detectable. For stability holds, confirmed-delay p90 may increase by at most a predeclared 0.75 s on timed positive controls, with no new misses. Also report suspected delay and maximum delay.

**Reject.** A reset treats every self-correction as a fresh mistake, creates duplicate warnings, or eliminates an error by associating it with the wrong occurrence. A timer-only delay that merely postpones the same false warnings is not progress.

**Risk.** Correctly detecting previously hidden later-occurrence errors may increase warning counts. Count correct and false warnings separately; do not demand universally fewer events. Timed real error sessions are currently scarce, so synthetic success alone is not release evidence.

### 5. Localize the actual divergent word and compare omission versus substitution explicitly

**Mechanism.** `flush()` converts a gap to wrong-word using the amount of decoded speech in it, and `scoreGap()` may cover several words. The issue then starts at `gap.word`, even when a matching prefix belongs to the preceding word. This fits the two human-reviewed فإن/الجحيم localization failures. Stray/borrowed neighboring phonemes can also turn a real omission into `wrong_word`, as at مع. These are diagnosis errors and matching failures, not necessarily false audible alerts.

**Small experiment.** Use the seven reviewed findings as development fixtures only. Within the same stable flank window, compare three hypotheses: intended words, true gap/omission, and a replacement localized after matching prefix/suffix context. Map edits back to word boundaries and retain a multiword internal span when needed. Evaluate exact class/location separately from whether a real mistake received any warning. Do not repair scores by relaxing benchmark matching.

**Minimal seam.** `scoreGap`/`flush` in `judge`, plus the internal `Issue` target range; change output location only when the evidence supports it. Add class-ambiguous diagnostics without inventing a new public event class in the first iteration.

**Success.** Fix the reviewed class/localization cases, preserve the audible-warning decision on real errors, and introduce no new mislocalizations in held-out wrong/omitted labels. A better F1 caused solely by reclassifying/relocalizing should be reported as such, separately from true clean-warning reduction.

**Reject.** A prefix-trimming rule shifts legitimate errors forward, assigns shared phonemes arbitrarily, or classifies listener noise as spoken replacement solely because the gap contains three tokens. Ambiguous audio needs independent review, not a convenient label change.

## Freeze the experiments before running them

1. Keep the 433-clip/98-speaker historical train/development partition for fitting; obtain matching student evidence if absent. Preserve speaker grouping. The seven human cases and the Luhaidan/Mandour assignment incidents are declared diagnostic development material. Do not call them held out after designing fixes around them.
2. Before any candidate replay, save one immutable evaluation manifest of evidence/model/reference hashes, positive IDs and context policy. Keep the remaining speaker-disjoint QUD recordings as a separate validation partition, and replay the existing 388-clip acceptance set once per frozen candidate as a regression set. That acceptance set has already informed past development; it is not a pristine scientific holdout. A final release claim still needs new speaker-disjoint, timed positive/clean learner sessions never used for tuning.
3. Use the identical evidence, time grid, context, finalized-warning definition and scorer across A/B runs. Report every positive loss and gain, class/location errors, `uncertain`/suspected-only counts, clean warnings/5 min, repeated-occurrence warnings and timed delays. Abstentions remain misses. Use per-speaker paired counts and speaker-cluster uncertainty intervals; do not treat words or overlapping chunks as independent samples. With only nine QUD speakers, intervals are descriptive and weak.
4. Compare each candidate against the unchanged baseline and simple global-threshold baselines at matched recall. Limit choices in advance, combine only successful independent ablations, then evaluate the combination once. If a validation result drives another design change, that partition becomes development material.
5. Stop before promotion if timed positive-session coverage cannot support recall and latency conclusions. No present clean-only QUD result proves a safe false-warning/recall tradeoff. On eventual implementation, any metric-affecting merge requires the repository's scoreboard command, an honest one-line note, and the updated scoreboard JSON.

## Primary research that informs these experiments

- [Naowarat et al., Word-level Confidence Estimation for CTC Models, Interspeech 2023](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2023/naowarat23b_interspeech.pdf): confidence estimation using surrounding sequence context outperformed naive CTC-softmax confidence on their speech tasks. Supports testing contextual confidence; does not validate a Quran threshold or make teacher agreement independent.
- [Laptev and Ginsburg, Fast Entropy-Based Methods of Word-Level Confidence Estimation](https://research.nvidia.com/publication/2024-01_fast-entropy-based-methods-word-level-confidence-estimation-end-end-automatic): a nontrained entropy-derived confidence alternative to maximum frame probability. Supports a cheap feature ablation using available posteriors. Its LibriSpeech results must not be projected onto Tilawa; truncated top-k rows also make entropy approximate.
- [Cao et al., A Framework for Phoneme-Level Pronunciation Assessment Using CTC, Interspeech 2024](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2024/cao24b_interspeech.pdf): evaluates context-preserving CTC hypothesis sets that include substitution/deletion/insertion and marginalize boundaries. This motivates controlled competing hypotheses, not a proposal to ship pronunciation scoring. Their table also shows that allowing every insertion/deletion alternative is not universally the best method; a larger hypothesis set is not automatically safer.

These are mechanism-level inspirations. The candidate rankings and numeric experiment targets above are engineering judgments based on this repository, not performance claims from those papers.
