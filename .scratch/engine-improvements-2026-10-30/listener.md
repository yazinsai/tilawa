# Listener and training recommendations — 30 October 2026

Research and code inspection only. No training, inference jobs, model downloads, label changes, or benchmark reruns. Numbers below are existing measurements; proposed gains and costs are hypotheses and estimates. Prioritize better supervision and matching the deployment conditions before changing the encoder.

## What is established

- The current checkpoint is the 64.6M-parameter, English-LibriSpeech-initialized `spliced-clean` Zipformer, trained on about 535 hours of tlog clean-match with CTC plus teacher top-5 KD. The checkpoint's gold PER is **4.71%**; the actual deployed `spliced-clean-int8` is **4.82%**, so these must not be interchanged. The deployment's phone slice is **10.07%, 26 clips**, laptop **10.66%, 16 clips**, tlog **3.14%, 191 clips**. The small phone slice is evidence of a problem, not a reliable population estimate. [Student v2](../../docs/benchmarks/student-v2.md), [current scoreboard](../../docs/benchmarks/scoreboard.md).
- The teacher scores 3.39% on pooled gold, but the coordinating diagnosis finds phone PER 9.12% and stitched-session false warnings 1.41/5 min versus student's 1.46. A larger or more faithfully copied teacher is therefore not, by itself, a solution to the product's false warnings. Targeted gains must be shown with the same tracker/checker and session policy.
- The spliced-reference target improves clean reference PER but restores the canonical sound on **41.7% of E1b splices**, versus **34.5%** for the teacher. Its greedy error retention is 43.4%. These are synthetic probes, not natural learner recall; E1b explicitly warns about seam artifacts and invalid directions. Current v1 supports memorization errors only. [E1b](../../docs/benchmarks/e1b-student-spliced-clean-v1.md), [ticket 08](../v1-roadmap/issues/08-student-distillation.md).
- The teacher's 8.51% raw reference PER decomposes into 4.25 points of edges, 2.15 of madd/ghunna length, and roughly 1.19 of likely mishearing. Raw text targets can teach hallucinated clip edges; blindly trusting intended text can also erase genuine errors. These diagnoses are heuristic, not independent acoustic truth. [Breakdown](../../docs/benchmarks/teacher-per-breakdown-v1.md).
- Current code applies noise/RIR/gain and SpecAugment, but no tempo/phone-channel augmentation. `load_clips` reads the teacher path, reference and top-5 arrays; the manifest already has cluster, confidence and PER, but the training loader does not retain confidence as a loss weight. KD uses one global fixed delay, and renormalizes the retained top-5 mass to one. CTC uses `zero_infinity=True`. Training periodically evaluates the split called `test`; it saves the final scheduled checkpoint rather than automatically picking the lowest test PER. Repeated test visibility still creates human model-selection leakage risk. [Loader and augmentation](../../modal/student_train.py), [loss and loop](../../modal/student_train.py), [manifest](../../tools/student_manifest.py), [KD](../../tools/student.py).

## Ranked experiments

### 1. Resolve target correctness before adding data or architecture

**Hypothesis:** Some apparent acoustic errors are bad supervision; some useful difficult cases are missing from clean-match training. Confidence is useful only after calibration against correctness, because a teacher can confidently canonicalize a real mistake.

First run the missing **2×2 ablation**: teacher-path versus spliced-reference CTC, crossed with KD weight 1 versus 2. Use identical gold-excluded manifests, initialization, steps, augmentation and seeds. Existing comparisons change target and weight together. Reuse existing two cells as historical references but rerun all four under one manifest for a causal comparison; use two seeds for any finalist. Do not add other changes to this grid.

Then build a 300–500-site training/development audit, stratified by phone/channel, pace, edges, vowels, emphatics, teacher/reference disagreement and confidence. Mine deviant clips **for review**, not as automatically correct text or teacher labels. Follow [audio-review.md](../../docs/agents/audio-review.md): reuse saved judgments, automate trace triage, calibrate an independent audio opinion if available, and leave only unresolved listening cases for humans. A second model agreeing with the teacher is correlated evidence, not ground truth.

Use the audit to compare uniform loss against a **correctness-aware target policy**: reviewed as-said labels override both sources; reviewed clean, well-anchored interiors can use text; uncertain spans get reduced teacher KD or are omitted from that loss, while useful clean context remains. Keep word omissions, substitutions, repeated words and realized vowel errors in as-said targets. Preserve difficult examples at a controlled rate instead of filtering every disagreement away. Keep intended passage and as-said transcript as separate fields. Start with a small reviewed reservoir, not a full-corpus relabel.

**Go/no-go:** the 2×2 identifies a reproducible clean-PER/error-retention tradeoff; the corrected-target arm subsequently improves independent dev phone PER by at least 5% relative or removes at least 20% of listener-attributed false-word incidents, with no more than 2 percentage points lost word-error recall. Report per-class counts, not just pooled PER. Reject a clean-PER gain bought by more canonicalized real errors. These thresholds are proposed decision rules, not promised effects.

**Estimated effort/cost:** 2–4 engineering days plus review; approximately $100–300 Modal for the grid, replay and confirmation runs. Prior full runs took 3–4 H100 hours at about $20/run in October logs; this estimate allows substantial overhead and is not a current price quote.

### 2. Teach errors explicitly: E3, then small real-learner adaptation

**Hypothesis:** Training on mostly correct Quran recitation rewards the encoder's textual prior. A bounded fraction of known as-said errors can restore discriminative acoustic evidence without rebuilding the model.

Create 5,000–20,000 training-only paired examples: letter+vowel swaps, short-vowel changes, omitted/added/repeated words, and separately controlled gemination. Begin with the known weak directions and v1 word errors. Pair every audio edit with a **same-seam correct control**, vary cut/crossfade details, and hold out splice recipes as well as donors. Use actual tlog/QUD train recordings; never reuse E1b's Husary/Minshawy evaluation recordings or donors.

The crucial loss rule: **the edited audio's target must say what the edited audio contains**. Do not run `spliced_target(original_reference, old_teacher)` on it. Turn off the old teacher KD around the modified sound and coarticulation boundary, or generate trusted new evidence there; unchanged context can retain KD. Teacher posteriors from the unedited waveform directly contradict the intended lesson.

Compare fixed-compute arms: clean control; 10% E3; E3 followed by a small real-learner adaptation stage; real-learner-only adaptation. Use roughly 200–500 reviewed training-speaker clips to start, with preserved clean replay to watch forgetting. Stage 2 is not accomplished by mixing everything together. Collect consented training speakers separately from an untouched evaluation group; there is no cash label budget, so volunteer/review capacity is the likely bottleneck.

**Evidence:** [Yang et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.24086v1) report QuranMB.v2 F1 0.6825 for staged single-checkpoint training, 0.6681 for real-only, 0.4629 for stage-1-only and 0.4305 for naive mixing. They use ~79h native, ~80h synthetic and ~2h learner speech. Their 300M encoder and six-model best system are not a phone proposal. [Iqra Extra](https://huggingface.co/datasets/IqraEval/Iqra_Extra_IS26) now requires accepting access conditions; its empty public card does not verify labels or permissions. Use existing private data/consented volunteers first, with any external data import audited separately.

**Go/no-go:** at least +10 percentage points E1b error retention on valid directions and reduced canonicalization toward/below the teacher's 34.5%, with stable same-seam controls; additionally +5 points real-error recall at fixed precision, or fewer listener-caused misses without extra clean warnings. Synthetic-only success does not justify a pronunciation release. Any supported pronunciation class still needs its own ≥80% precision gate; retain separate memorization acceptance.

**Estimated effort/cost:** 4–8 engineering/review days; $100–500 Modal for building/replaying examples and 3–6 adaptation runs. Volunteer recruitment/review time is additional, not purchased with compute credits.

### 3. Match phone and fast-recitation conditions, with correct targets

**Hypothesis:** targeted channel/pace coverage adds more value than another undifferentiated 500 expert hours. First inspect fast-session incidents jointly with the tracker team: a valid different Hafs convention, incorrect timing/reference boundary, or state error is not an acoustic training label.

Pilot 50–100 additional hours with balanced phone/learner material and selected fast expert recitation. Choose by measured phones/second and reviewed output, not style name alone. [Muaalem data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/obadx/muaalem-annotated-v3) exposes recitation-speed and Hafs-convention metadata; its [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.00094v2) reports 848h from 22 experts, but premium studio speech is not phone or learner diversity. [QUD](https://huggingface.co/datasets/QUD-Technologies/quranic-universal-ayahs) supplies word/letter timing and source offsets; preserve its held-out recordings. Deduplicate across sources before claiming new hours.

Compare matched-compute baseline with separately added channel augmentation and tempo augmentation. Candidates: measured band limitation, compression/clipping, modest time stretching (for example 0.85–1.2×) and the current noise/RIR bank. Modest synthetic acceleration is not evidence of natural hadr competence. On time-warped audio, warp frame targets consistently or re-run the teacher; never reuse unchanged KD frame indices. Keep natural rhythm, word-boundary and pausing slices separate.

Audit CTC feasibility at fast rates: output frames must accommodate target phones plus adjacent repeats. Log impossible/zeroed CTC cases, per-phone occupancy and deletion rate before changing frame rate. At present `zero_infinity=True` can hide an impossible target; there is no evidence yet that this occurs. Only if reviewed rapid phones are being lost despite feasible correct targets should a 50Hz auxiliary/local branch or changed subsampling be considered. Missing shadda must be measured separately because the headline canonical PER collapses repeats.

**Go/no-go:** at least 10% relative phone-PER gain and improvement on fresh natural fast-recitation recordings, without >2-point word-error recall loss or worse false warnings on ordinary pace. Require several held-out fast speakers and minute-scale clean sessions; do not unmark hadr unsupported from artificial speed tests alone.

**Estimated effort/cost:** 3–6 engineering days; $150–800 including new-source teacher passes, several full runs and session replay. Start with the 50–100h pilot; expand only after a visible held-out gain.

### 4. Fix distillation timing and posterior information only where measured

**Hypothesis:** offline teacher spikes and causal student spikes differ in time; same 25Hz frame rate does not establish matching emission times. This can penalize a correct delayed student. It is separate from the teacher being wrong.

Inspect about 200 reviewed dev clips for lag by phoneme, pace, clip edge and chunk phase. Compare current fixed-delay KD with a small monotonic bounded alignment window (0/40/80ms), keeping weights, targets and steps fixed. The existing `kd_delay` can test fixed offsets first. Do not let an unconstrained per-frame minimum jump between different phones or cross the known synthetic-error region.

Also measure retained top-5 probability mass and blank/nonblank loss contribution. Only if these show a material problem, compare a residual-`other` mass objective or selective full-43-class posterior pilot against renormalization; compare blank-balanced KD as a distinct ablation. Do not assume top-5 truncation is costly when retained mass is already near one. The current files retain original top-5 probabilities, allowing the mass audit without a new teacher pass.

**Evidence:** [Delayed-KD](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.22069v1) reports AISHELL streaming CER 7.02% at zero allowance and 6.11% with an 80ms alignment window; first-token median delay increases 100→140ms. The paper uses Mandarin and a different encoder/decoder, so neither its gain nor its latency transfers to Tilawa. A training alignment window adds no necessary inference parameter count, but learned emission delay still must be measured.

**Go/no-go:** at least 5% relative dev phone-PER improvement or a clear reduction in listener-attributed boundary misses; stable real-error retention; deployed highlight/confirmation latency within product limits. If peak lags are negligible or inconsistent, skip this arm.

**Estimated effort/cost:** 2–4 engineering days; $50–200 for audit, small pilots and one full confirmation.

### 5. Distill the improved full model into lite; leave larger architectures last

The 22.3M lite currently starts from scratch and is worse on both deployed gold PER (5.74%) and short-clip false warnings (1.081/5min versus full 0.433 in that comparison). Once full improves, distill it to lite with the **same reviewed error targets**, replay reservoir and deployment tests. A teacher assistant is plausible because both are causal and share the inventory; it is unproven here. Keep real-error labels authoritative so better clean recognition does not become stronger canonicalization.

Do export parity first: matched waveform through masked PyTorch, stateful fp32 export and final int8 with varied chunk boundaries and long continuous audio. Current export documentation itself notes that chunk-masked and stateful computation can differ. Current int8 quantizes MatMul weights; its pooled gold delta from fp32 is only about 0.11 points, so quantization is unlikely the main 5–6-point phone-domain gap. Examine per-class divergences before trying selectively unquantized output layers or quantization-aware training. [Export](../../modal/student_export.py), [quantization](../../tools/quantize_onnx.py), [scoreboard](../../docs/benchmarks/scoreboard.md).

**Go/no-go:** at least 10% relative lite phone-PER improvement with stable mistake recall and real-time performance on the Android floor, near the existing ~25MB artifact budget. Reject speed/size wins that materially increase false words. Estimated 2–4 days and $50–200.

E6 is a later, small auxiliary-head experiment if local confusions survive correct supervision: feature labels must describe the realized sound, not derive solely from canonical text. [Multi-view multi-task research](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.01845v2) supports testing articulatory supervision, not assuming its gain or deploying two large encoders. General Arabic pretraining, a new 300M model, dense optimal-transport objectives and ensembling have higher implementation/domain risks and should follow these cheaper tests. [Prompt-free MDD](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.22133v1) independently demonstrates that lower PER can accompany substantially worse error recall when canonical prompts are supplied; use this as motivation for dual gates, not proof about our CTC implementation.

## Shared evaluation and budget discipline

1. Freeze a dev manifest and a new final test before any sweep. Split by person and original recording across tlog, QUD, Muaalem and mirrors; approximate speaker clusters need audit. Keep every gold ID excluded. Retain the 62-clip historical uncontaminated subset for old-model comparisons. Use a passage-held-out challenge slice in addition to normal repeated-Quran evaluation, to expose context memorization.
2. Existing gold/help/E1b have already informed choices. Treat them as regression sets; add independent held-out learner and fast speakers for confirmation. Start with 5–10 evaluation volunteers and 1–2h total covering clean minutes, genuine errors, scripted errors and self-correction. This is a pilot; it does not guarantee class-level statistical power. Keep volunteer training and evaluation people disjoint. Store original audio, heard transcript, intended passage, phonetic convention and uncertainty separately.
3. Score deployed streaming int8 as well as checkpoint: as-said PER, canonical PER for continuity, word-error visibility/retention, per-direction pronunciation recall at fixed precision, clean false warnings/minute, location coverage and first/last-word latency. Report clip/site counts and bootstrap paired differences by speaker, not treating every frame as independent. Hold the tracker/checker version and thresholds fixed to isolate listener changes; then measure end-to-end effects.
4. Score word omissions/additions/repeats separately from phone-class and gemination mistakes. A listener should preserve the heard wrong word; deciding whether a correct alternative Quran passage is a memorization error depends on the declared task. Training the listener to emit the intended passage would hide that distinction.
5. Run serial decision gates, not the Cartesian product of all suggestions. A sensible first allocation is **$500–1,500**, with a **$3,000 maximum for this initial programme** including retries and confirmation; leave most of the ~$20k credits unused until real gains appear. Estimates are based on local historical run costs, not a live Modal quote. Data correctness and new human evidence, rather than GPU supply, are the limiting resources.
6. A future metric-moving implementation must append the required scoreboard row and provenance; this research-only note does not change the scoreboard. None of the proposed experiments guarantees gains or a pronunciation release.
