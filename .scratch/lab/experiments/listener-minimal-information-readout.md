## Implemented mechanism

`modal/student_bottleneck_export.py` exposes the effective final CTC projection
input in a separate copy of the shipped ONNX. Dynamic-quantized inputs are
reconstructed from their activation codes, scales and zero points. Encoder
weights, normal outputs and recurrent caches remain unchanged. The exporter
checks exact equality across eight sequential inference windows and verifies
the extracted projection numerically. Those model-backed checks have not run.

The SDK utility caches full-width deployment features from eligible help-train
training speakers' none-labelled recordings. It rejects excluded takes, edits,
repeats and non-training splits; a speaker with any non-training label is
excluded globally. An additional speaker-exclusion list is supported. Release
held-out speakers are absent from the authorized mount. Canonical transcripts
come from the generic reference index, not engine predictions. No practice
recording supplies training supervision or model-selection input.

For normalized frame features x, the variational encoder produces a 32-coordinate
mean μ(x) and diagonal log variance v(x), bounded to [−8, 4]. Its Gaussian latent
q(z|x) is scored by a linear full-vocabulary phone head. Training minimizes
sampled transcript CTC plus β times KL(q(z|x) || N(0,I)), summed over latent
coordinates and averaged over valid frames. The prior is shared across all
phones, frames and speakers. Padded frames are excluded from CTC lengths and
KL; the frozen encoder's features are generated by separate streaming replay.

The deterministic and fixed-noise controls have the same encoder and output
matrix dimensions and exactly the same number of actively learned parameters:
2L(D+1) + V(L+1), for feature width D, latent width L and vocabulary size V.
They use both encoder branches to form a mean μ(x) + tanh(v(x)); the fixed-noise
arm adds independent Gaussian noise of fixed standard deviation exp(−1).
In the variational arm the second branch instead learns conditional variance.
This avoids matching capacity with unused variance parameters. It matches
parameter count, rather than making the controls' deterministic transform
identical to the variational mean, which limits causal isolation.

All arms share cached features, normalization, initialization, recording order,
update count and optimizer settings. The noisy arms use common random numbers.
The predeclared β grid is 0.001, 0.01 and 0.1; checkpoints are 20, 40 and 80
epochs. Three whole-speaker folds are assigned before normalization or fitting.
Selection minimizes held-training-speaker CTC per phone plus lexical
preservation error. These controls compare recorded canonical pronunciations
against single-phone lexical counterfactual references selected from the generic
mushaf lexicon. They check retention of acoustic distinctions, but are not
performed transcripts of scripted mistake takes. The selected β and shared
checkpoint are refitted on all eligible training speakers. The submitted
pipeline allows 2700 seconds of training, within a sixty-minute remote run and
the one-day experiment budget; a timeout publishes no fitted model.

`packages/sdk/src/bottleneck.ts` integrates frame probabilities over the learned
Gaussian using deterministic third-degree spherical-radial quadrature (2L
points). Its nodes reproduce the distribution's mean and covariance exactly;
the nonlinear softmax integral remains approximate. Probabilities are
integrated before full-pronunciation CTC, rather than averaging logits or
scoring only the latent mean. All arms use identical native frame windows and
a completion-conditioned shared boundary prior, marginalizing legal paths and
requiring blank separation between repeated labels.

The checker exposes only the already-supported complete written/rival strings
and existing acoustic bounds for ordinary wrong-word confirmations. The
auxiliary scorer supplies a certificate only when the bottleneck strictly
prefers written and both controls strictly prefer rival. Invalid, tied,
nonfinite, incomplete or out-of-inventory comparisons abstain. Certificates
carry all six scores and match the exact strings, location and frame bounds.
The checker preserves its internal confirmation and ownership ledger and
changes only the public verdict. Graph confirmations are exempt. Session review
uses otherwise identical baseline/certified replays to downgrade only the exact
live occurrence. Primary tokens, confidence, posteriors, tracker events and
insertion decisions are preserved by construction and have dedicated tests.

The normal Node loader validates both declared side files by size and SHA-256,
checks readout geometry and binds the readout to the exact primary hash.
Missing or corrupt declared assets fail loading. Packaging copies the primary
model, manifest and existing teacher assets unchanged. An SDK verification mode
checks Python/TypeScript probability parity through the normal bundle loader.
No fitted bundle or model selection has been declared in this worktree.

Review currently happens at audio-session completion and retains session PCM
and evidence for a separate full encoder replay. A streaming warning may be
issued before its later downgrade. Device memory, latency, Gaussian quadrature
accuracy and preservation of genuine substitutions are not established.
