# Weight-consistent training cache audit

Status: killed on the premise; no engine change retained.

## Prediction

Listener −1 and overall −1 wrong calls, with one recovered addition and no
predicted losses. The hypothesis was that training consumes acoustic states
computed before an optimizer update, so weak consonants are learned against
context generated by different parameter versions.

## Proposed mechanism

Version retained acoustic states by the weights that produced them. Before
consuming a stale state, recompute its retained acoustic prefix using current
weights, preserving context length, chunk geometry and gradient detachment.
Only if such stale consumption exists, compare a bounded upper-block refit with
a frozen stem against an identical-data, identical-update-count control.

## Premise audit

The actual training path has no carried acoustic state:

- `modal/student_train.py:134`: `Student.forward(feats, feat_lens)` recomputes
  the subsampling stem and calls the ordinary `Zipformer2.forward` on the whole
  batch. It receives neither incoming states nor an acoustic cache.
- `modal/student_train.py:306`: `losses` calls that forward once for the current
  batch. CTC and KD consume the resulting current-weight outputs.
- `modal/student_train.py:460`: each batch finishes `losses`, backward and
  `opt.step()` before advancing. No computed acoustic prefix or encoder state
  is passed to the following batch. The `state` dictionary stores epoch, batch,
  step and skipped-loss counters, not acoustic tensors. Checkpoint resume also
  restores parameters and optimizer/scheduler state without acoustic tensors.
- `docs/benchmarks/student-v2.md` identifies this training recipe as the source
  of `spliced-clean`. Its streaming training context is expressed by chunk and
  left-context masks, not by calling a stateful streaming encoder between
  optimizer updates.
- `modal/student_export.py:44` defines the distinct inference wrapper that
  calls `streaming_forward` with explicit attention/convolution states, stem
  padding and processed lengths. Export and decoding use evaluation mode and
  fixed weights; that stateful loop does not perform optimizer updates.

The in-memory FLAC, teacher top-five distributions, augmentation banks and
optimizer moments are not student acoustic context computed by trainable
layers. Updating them would not instantiate the card's mechanism.

## Result and limits

The card's first kill condition applies: training does not consume acoustic
states from an older parameter version. No cache implementation, matched refit
or new model export is warranted. Adding stateful chunk-by-chunk training would
introduce a different training regime rather than correct existing cache reuse.

Recovered targets: **0**. Practice scores requested: **0**. This is a structural
premise result, not a measured practice delta or a claim that a different
training regime could never help the listener. No practice or help-train audio
was used for training or checkpoint selection.

No engine or unit-test behavior was changed. The experiment card, scorer, lab
tooling, manifests and evidence remain unchanged, and no model override is
declared.
