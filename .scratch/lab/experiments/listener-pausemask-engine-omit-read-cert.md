# Whole-session pause-mask counting reads for omission doubts

Status: investigating the counting-visit premise; no practice score requested.

## Prediction

The card predicts one or two of three targeted omission false warnings downgraded
to uncertainty, listener −1 and overall −1. The implementation brief's later
stop rule requires three target wins before scoring, overriding the card's
weaker minimum of one overlapping counting read (including 4:15:6).

## Mechanism and early falsification

The proposed listener certificate is a positive read: the separately trained
pause-mask int8 student drives its own complete listener, free tracker and
checker, and supplies zero-hard-edit counting visits with native frame bounds.
Only an existing single-candidate primary omission in the same locked ayah and
overlapping anchored stretch may become uncertain. Merely failing to accuse a
word supplies no certificate; two-candidate omission placements are excluded.
No primary token, posterior row, tracker decision or numeric cut is changed.

Before building certificate transport or a veto, a temporary read-only checker
observer records **every** counting visit of the complete second engine,
including provisional alignments. It uses the unchanged counting conditions
(coverage, consumed phones and kept consonants), and reports the visit's hard
edits and native frame interval. Deduplicated visits are retained across the
whole run. This is deliberately more permissive than the proposed terminal
certificate: absence of a zero-hard-edit visit anywhere also rules out an
eligible final visit in the narrower anchored stretch. Positive upper-bound
visits alone do not establish target wins.

The diagnostic reuses the native mask input and detector geometry preserved in
implementation commit `09ff3a42`. It first runs the pause-mask student over the
entire audio unmasked, discovers pauses and tracker steps from that engine's
own evidence, then runs the entire audio again with the resulting masks. The
ordinary gap decode and folded teacher insertion path remain enabled. No labels,
primary omission sites or primary window bounds enter either side-engine run.

previous whole-session experiment was requested explicitly before reading any

not an acoustic kill and not evidence of zero target wins.
